## What BridgeTO is

BridgeTO Youth is a Toronto non-profit that gives underserved young people programs, mentorship and a community to grow in. I started volunteering as a mentor in 2020, became marketing lead in 2021, and now serve as tech director.

## The hackathons

Twice a year we partner with Amazon to run a hackathon for underserved students. BridgeTO Hacks: Your Voice is Power, our edition of the **Your Voice is Power** program, was co-hosted with Amazon Future Engineer and TakingITGlobal. Students learn Python by remixing music: they write code that lays down beats, layers samples and builds a track. The day runs like a playlist, with six coding modules, talks from guest speakers, a working session, judging, and winners, and the strongest remixes go to a final round. One of our top 6 finalists' remixes became the background music for [our thank-you video](https://www.facebook.com/BridgeTOyouthCanada/videos/501564617721159/) to the teachers who joined us.

## What I do

- **Create the curriculum.** I design the lessons and starter projects students use to build their remix, so every concept pays off in something they can hear.
- **Teach.** I teach the coding sessions and help teams when they get stuck.
- **Run the event.** I help run the hackathons with Amazon and our partners.
- **Mentor.** I've mentored BridgeTO students since 2020, well beyond the hackathons.

## Why music

A for loop is abstract until you hear it repeat a drum pattern. Music gives students instant, physical feedback on their code, and it lets them bring their own taste into the room, which matters for kids who have never been told that computer science is for them.
