## The problem

Your life is spread across a calendar, a to-do list, notes, inboxes and a dozen open tabs. Finding one thing means first remembering which app it lives in, and starting your day means opening five of them.

## What we built

Scout was a unified search engine for personal data. One search bar sits on top of everything, and the home screen gathers what you need to start the day: your calendar for today and tomorrow, a focus timer, the news that's relevant to you and a quote to start on. Widgets can be added and removed, so the page fits how each person works.

## What I did

I co-founded Scout with [Eric Chen](https://ericdachen.com/design) and worked on the product and design from idea to MVP. We interviewed with **Y Combinator, Neo, Pear VC and Antler**.

## What I learned

A dashboard that does a bit of everything is easy to like and hard to need. Since Scout, the first question I ask about any product is which single job is painful enough that someone will switch tools for it.
